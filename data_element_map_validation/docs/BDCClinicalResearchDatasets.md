# Current Clinical Research Datasets

The clinical research cohorts currently available for analysis. All are controlled-access (dbGaP); availability to query depends on approved Data Access Requests per study and consent group.

| Abbrev. | Study | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **ARIC** | Atherosclerosis Risk in Communities | Prospective cardiovascular cohort across four U.S. communities |
| **CARDIA** | Coronary Artery Risk Development in Young Adults | Development of CVD risk from young adulthood onward |
| **CHS** | Cardiovascular Health Study | CVD risk factors in adults aged 65 and older |
| **COPDGene** | Genetic Epidemiology of COPD | COPD and emphysema genetics, with chest CT imaging |
| **FHS** | Framingham Heart Study | Multi-generational cardiovascular cohort |
| **HCHS/SOL** | Hispanic Community Health Study / Study of Latinos | Multi-system health in Hispanic/Latino adults |
| **JHS** | Jackson Heart Study | Cardiovascular disease in African American adults |
| **MESA** | Multi-Ethnic Study of Atherosclerosis | Subclinical CVD across multiple ethnic groups |
| **SPIROMICS** | SubPopulations and InteRmediate Outcome Measures In COPD Study | COPD subphenotyping and intermediate outcomes |
| **WHI** | Women's Health Initiative | Postmenopausal women's CVD, cancer, and osteoporosis |

---

## Notes

- **Pulmonary cohorts:** COPDGene and SPIROMICS are the two dedicated COPD studies in this set — the most relevant for respiratory/emphysema phenotype work.
- **Cross-study harmonization:** these cohorts overlap heavily with the TOPMed DCC harmonized phenotype set, so harmonized variable definitions are available across most of them in PIC-SURE.
- **Accessions:** confirm exact, versioned phs accessions in dbGaP before use — e.g. ARIC `phs000280`, COPDGene `phs000179` (parent) / `phs000951` (TOPMed), FHS `phs000007` (parent) / `phs000974` (TOPMed).
